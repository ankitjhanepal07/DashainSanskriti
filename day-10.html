DAY 10 • GRAND FINALE

Vijaya Dashami

Vijaya Dashami is the culmination of the ten-day Dashain journey and is traditionally associated with family gatherings, blessings and celebration.

For many families, this is the day when loved ones come together, receive tika and jamara according to their traditions, exchange blessings and create memories together.
